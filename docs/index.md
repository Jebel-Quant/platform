# A Technology Vision for Quantitative Trading

*Thomas Schmelzer, Jebel Quant Research — May 2026*

Jebel Quant Research builds the tools and infrastructure for systematic trading teams —
from data access and portfolio construction to live execution and repo management.
This document sets out the thinking behind that work.

It covers the problems with the traditional research-to-production handover, the case
for a shared environment, the kitchen analogy for team structure, and the role of
containerisation, backtesting discipline, risk management and AI in a modern quant
operation.
